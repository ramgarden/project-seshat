# Raxxla Search Criteria — Community Handbook

This document captures what the community looks for when systematically searching for
Raxxla, distilled from The Great Raxxla Potato Hunt (GRPH) and the elite-dangerous lore
wiki. The in-app search guide uses these hints to flag "worth investigating" targets.

## The search area

- The **Great Raxxla Potato Hunt** surveys **every star system within 200 ly of Sol** (~68,000 systems), divided into **20 ly cubes**. (Fandom: *Great Raxxla Potato Hunt*; sites.google.com/view/greatraxxlapotatohunt; Frontier forum thread 531480.)
- Because Frontier's Michael Brookes said the Raxxla system has **"been visited at least once"** and is reachable by older/primitive ships, the community concentrates near the home bubble. (Fandom Raxxla notes; clip of Lavecon Q&A.)
- David Braben confirmed Raxxla **is in the game** and Frontier "know where it is". (Fandom Raxxla notes.)

## Lore hooks / search themes

- **Raxxla** = a legendary place holding a secret; the **Omphalos Rift** = a gateway/tunnel to other universes associated with it. "Omphalos" = navel/centre (Delphi connection). (Fandom Raxxla.)
- **The Dark Wheel** = the fraternity dedicated to finding Raxxla; their "toast". (In-game codex / Fandom.)
- **Princess Astrophel and the Spiralling Stars** — a children's story said to conceal hints. (Fandom Raxxla.)
- **"How to see Raxxlan aliens / hyperspace"** threads and the "witch-space whisperer" theme tie Raxxla to **hyperconjunctions/flight paths and mysterious signals**. (Frontier forum thread 623489.)

## What to look for (signals & bodies)

Per the GRPH "Getting Started" doc (search procedure):

1. **FSS the whole system** (ignore Transient Signal Sources, far-left).
2. **Nav Panel scan** — write down anything "unusual":
   - `Other` signal sources
   - Tourist Beacons
   - Megaships
   - **Unregistered Comms Beacons** (UCCs)
   - Convoy Beacons
   - Wakes / anomalies
3. **System Map check** — look for landing locations/features registering as **Other, Guardian, Thargoid**; human settlements vs. crashed ships mismatches.
4. **8th moons of gas giants** — the project explicitly asks that any 8th moon be mapped. (Echoes the Dark Wheel HQ being on "the eighth moon of an unnamed gas giant".) (GRPH doc; Fandom Raxxla / Steam community theory.)
5. **Out-of-the-ordinary body types / planet classes** — anything that "makes you go hmm": rare/odd classes, unusually-ranked systems, permit-looking bodies.

## Notable body classes (DSS-worthy / suspicious)

The existing search guide already treats these as worth DSS; for Raxxla intel treat these as higher-signal:

- Earth-like worlds (esp. terraformables)
- Water worlds (terraformable water-worlds track almost equal to ELWs)
- Ammonia worlds
- Terraformable high-metal-content / rocky bodies
- Bodies with **non-standard classifications** or unusual composition
- 8th moons of any gas giant (the Dark Wheel clue)

## Notable signal types (FSS "interesting" set)

The guide's `SignalTypes` come from `FSSSignalsFound`. For Raxxla intel, flag signals that match community "unusual" categories:

- `Other` (generic/unknown)
- `Non-Human` / `Thargoid` / `Guardian` / `Mechan` provenance
- `Unregistered` / `Wakes` / `Anomaly`
- Anything not clearly `Biological`/`Geological`/`Human`/`Technology`

## Lore names worth flagging in system/body names

Match on substrings (case-insensitive) to surface low-probability-but-cheap hits:

- `Raxxla`, `Omphalos`, `Rift`, `Dark Wheel`, `Astrophel`, `Spiralling`
- `Jewel`, `Whisperer`, `Siren`, `Void`, `Mother`, `Galaxies` (toast terms)
- `Delphi` (Greek navel-of-the-world pun), `Witchspace`, `Hyper confinement`
